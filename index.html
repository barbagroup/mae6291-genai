Generative AI for Engineering Research

Leverage AI as a research accelerator: practical tools to enhance PhD research productivity.

About

This course teaches engineering PhD students to use generative AI to accelerate their research rather than to build AI systems. It covers literature-review assistance, agentic research workflows, and AI-enhanced coding, alongside enough foundations—neural networks, transformers, tokenization, embeddings, retrieval-augmented generation—to use these tools effectively and critically.
